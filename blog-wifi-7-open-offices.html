Why Wi-Fi 7 is a Game Changer for Open Offices

Aug 15, 2026ITServe Network Team4 min read

An open office asks a lot of its wireless network. Video calls, cloud apps, guest devices, meeting-room displays, and phones all compete for attention in the same airspace. Wi-Fi 7 is designed to make that busy environment more predictable.

More capacity where it matters

Wi-Fi 7 improves how access points schedule traffic and use available spectrum. Rather than simply chasing a headline speed number, the standard is useful because it reduces congestion when dozens of devices make small, frequent requests at once.

Multi-Link Operation changes the conversation

Compatible devices can use more than one band with Multi-Link Operation. That gives the network another route around interference and can improve latency for voice, video, and interactive work.

  • Better performance in dense meeting areas and shared desks.
  • Lower latency for calls, collaboration, and cloud desktops.
  • More efficient use of 2.4 GHz, 5 GHz, and 6 GHz spectrum.
  • Improved capacity for managed devices and guest access.
A Wi-Fi 7 upgrade is most effective after a wireless survey. Placement, cabling, power, and access policies matter as much as the access point.

Plan the upgrade around your workforce

Not every device must be replaced on day one. A phased rollout can support current Wi-Fi 6 and 6E equipment while preparing new laptops and phones for Wi-Fi 7. Start with the areas where dropped calls or crowded connections affect people most.

Measure the employee experience

Use monitoring to compare coverage, roaming, latency, and application performance before and after deployment. Those measurements turn a network project into a clear business improvement rather than a hardware refresh.
