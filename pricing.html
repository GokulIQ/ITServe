Annual Maintenance Contracts

A Computer AMC is a 12-month support agreement for the listed devices at your home or business. It combines scheduled preventive checks with help for faults during the contract period.

How to read these plans: Each AMC runs for 12 months. Choose annual or monthly billing below; monthly billing changes the payment schedule, not the contract term. Prices apply to the listed device limit. Service and troubleshooting are included as listed; replacement parts are excluded unless the quotation explicitly includes them. Response times are targets to acknowledge a request, not guaranteed repair completion times.
